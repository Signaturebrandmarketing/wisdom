One prompt you paste into Claude. It interviews you about your vision, then builds a page you can share as a link.

How it works

  1. Copy the prompt belowTap the gold Copy button.
  2. Open Claude and start a new chatOn claude.ai or in the Claude app. Paste the prompt and send it.
  3. Answer Claude's questionsYour goal, your why, your scripture, your streams and your story. Give yourself about 15 to 20 minutes.
  4. Look it over and make changesTell Claude what to change, in plain words, until it feels like you.
  5. Publish it and share your linkUse the Publish or Share button on the page Claude built.

The prompt

Copy all of this

Help me build my personal Vision Page: one beautiful, mobile-friendly web page I can share as a link. Think of it as a living vision board for my life and my work. I'm part of the Wisdom Is Currency cohort. Make the page faith-centered, with God at the center.

STEP 1: INTERVIEW ME FIRST
Ask me these questions one or two at a time. Wait for my answers, keep it conversational, and ask a follow-up if an answer is thin. Don't build anything until we've covered them all.
1. My name as I want it on the page, and a title for my vision (for example, "Five Streams, One River").
2. My big goal: what I'm building toward (an income number, an impact goal, or both) and my timeframe.
3. My "why": who this is for and why it matters to me (family, legacy, calling).
4. My keystone scripture or guiding verse.
5. My streams: the businesses, projects, skills or income sources that make up my vision (usually 3 to 8). For each one: its name, one sentence on what it is, and where it stands today (running, building, ready to activate, or still an idea).
6. Whether I want to leave one stream open for whatever God may bring that I haven't thought of yet.
7. A story or everyday moment when my plan clicked for me.
8. My operating rule: the one thing I must do (or stop doing) to make this real.
9. A daily affirmation or prayer I speak over this vision.
If I share a photo of a handwritten plan or vision board, read it carefully and use it as source material.

STEP 2: BUILD THE PAGE
Make it one self-contained HTML page (an artifact) with these sections, in this order:
- Hero: my name, my vision title, my big goal, my timeframe and my operating rule, with my keystone scripture.
- The Why: my reason in large, simple type, with three short supporting points.
- The Living Map: an interactive circle diagram with God at the center and my streams around it. Tapping a stream (or the center) shows its description and status.
- The Streams: one card per stream with a number, its name, its description and a colored status label. If I chose an open stream, give it its own card.
- The Guiding Image: my story, told in a few short paragraphs.
- The Mandate: my operating rule as a bold closing statement.
- The Daily Affirmation, in large type at the very end.

Design: elegant and premium. Use a deep near-black background, warm gold accents and soft cream text, Playfair Display for headings and Inter for text, and green, amber and blue for the status labels. Make sure it looks great on a phone. Use my real words and keep my voice. Don't invent facts, numbers, names or quotes I didn't give you.

STEP 3: FINISH
Show me the page and ask what I'd like to change. When I'm happy, remind me to:
- remove anything private before I share it (exact income, addresses, account details, other people's personal information), and
- use the Publish or Share button to get my link.

If the Copy button doesn't work on your phone, tap inside the box. It selects the whole prompt, so you can copy it from there.

Tips

  • Have a handwritten plan or vision board? Take a photo and add it to the chat. Claude will read it and use it.
  • Answer in your own words. Long answers are fine. You can talk instead of type if your phone has voice typing.
  • Before you share your link, take out anything private: exact income, addresses, account details, or anyone else's personal information.
  • Bring your page to a Thursday gathering. We'd love to see it.
