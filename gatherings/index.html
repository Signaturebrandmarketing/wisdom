Gatherings

Thursdays at 7 PM Eastern.

One night a week on Zoom with Ken Bridgett and a growing community of believers and builders. Free, open to anyone, and recorded in case you miss it.

Join freeMembers: get the link

What happens on a Thursday

Every gathering follows the same simple shape, so you always know what you’re walking into.

  1. We open the room

    A prayer and short introductions, so the newest person on the call gets heard, not just the regulars.

  2. The teaching

    One piece of the framework, taught from Scripture in plain language: the wheel, the five accounts, the tithe of time, getting the vision out of your head.

  3. Your turn

    Questions, testimonies and what you’re building. This is where the room helps you think.

  4. We close in prayer

    With one assignment to carry into the week, so the teaching goes home with you.
