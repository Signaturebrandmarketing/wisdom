Wisdom Is Currency

Creating a Cycle of Prosperity in Your Life, by Kenneth Lamor Bridgett

Most of us were taught two lies: that wanting money is unspiritual, and that having ideas is the same as building them. This book breaks both.

Join freeMembers hear first when it’s out.

What it’s about

Wisdom is a currency. You can spend it, invest it and earn it, and God gives it so you can provide for your family, prosper as a whole person and draw closer to Him in the process.

This isn’t a get-rich book. It’s a system, a Cycle of Prosperity, that turns time spent with God into wisdom, wisdom into ideas, ideas into value, and value into a life that’s free.

Who it’s for

Believers working through money and purpose. Readers who are curious about faith and want it explained plainly; every Scripture is summarized so anyone can follow. And visionaries with more ideas than hours, who keep getting stuck between the vision and the execution.

Ten chapters

Each chapter is one turn of the same wheel, told through Ken’s story and anchored in Scripture.

  1. The Principal Thing

    Wisdom is a currency, and money is downstream of it. Proverbs 4:7

  2. You Are the Asset

    Your worth is the raw material God multiplies. Psalm 139:14

  3. The Tithe of Time

    Master time the way a believer masters money. Proverbs 3:9

  4. The Cycle of Prosperity

    The wheel that turns time into wisdom, value and more time. Deuteronomy 8:18

  5. Clock In

    Showing up is not the same as being paid for it. Luke 10:7

  6. The Conversion Season

    Enduring the gap between the idea and the harvest. Habakkuk 2:3

  7. The Whole Man

    Real wealth is balance across five accounts. 3 John 1:2

  8. Your Signature DNA

    Capture the real you so your work can scale, with AI as a mirror. James 1:23-24

  9. Out of Your Head

    Turning a flood of ideas into a plan you can run. Habakkuk 2:2

  10. The Perpetual System

    Leverage, generosity and building what outlasts you. Luke 6:38

Plus an introduction, “The Day I Clocked In,” and a closing word.

The ten chapters are also the ten modules of the Wisdom Is Currency Mastermind, where the book gets lived out together over a year. See how to go deeper
