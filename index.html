Wisdom is the currency. Wealth is what it buys.

A free weekly gathering where believers and builders learn how time with God becomes wisdom, and wisdom becomes wealth.

Join free Read the framework

The world has shifted, and it is still shifting. How people earn, work, build and provide is being remade in real time. Nobody should face that unprepared. We would rather be led through the shift than left behind by it.

You don’t have one account. You have five.

Most of us were taught to watch one number. Here, wealth means balance across all five accounts, because a full bank account can’t make up for an empty life.

We start with one. We build all five.

How the five accounts work

The five accountsWhat it holds
  1. God

    The relationship everything else flows from. Not religion. Nearness.

  2. Health

    The body you carry it all in, and the one thing you can’t replace.

  3. Relationships

    Marriage, children, friends, and the people who carry you when you can’t carry yourself.

  4. Time freedom

    Margin. The freedom to be present without a clock pulling you away.

  5. Finances

    The money. Real, necessary, and last on the list.

Wealth is the balance between all five.

Four ways we build together

What you learn on Thursday is meant to show up in your business, your budget and your community.

  • Start & Grow

    Launch and grow a business on wisdom principles, with today’s tools taught plainly by someone who builds with them every day.

  • Steward

    Biblical money management: budgeting, getting out of debt and thinking in generations. Not just making money, but keeping it and passing it down.

  • Skill Up

    Practical skills for today’s economy, AI included, that raise what your work is worth, whether you’re employed or building your own.

  • Circulate

    Members doing business with each other, so money turns over inside the community before it leaves.
