Vision Execution System

We build it with you.

You already have the vision. What’s been missing is execution. Over ninety days we get everything out of your head, map it, and build the systems that carry it, with you at the table the whole way.

  1. Month one

    Focus

    Get crystal clear on the vision, map the plan and the priorities, and lock the foundation: your brand, your offer and how you’re positioned.

  2. Month two

    Build

    We build the assets and systems with you: brand, funnels, content and automation, with weekly working sessions to keep it moving.

  3. Month three

    Execute

    Launch to your market, refine what’s working, cut what isn’t, and walk away with a system that keeps running.

Built for visionaries with more ideas than hours, including the ADHD-wired, who are tired of ten things started and two finished.

Wisdom Is Currency Mastermind

The book is the map. The Mastermind is the walk.

A year inside the framework with a small circle of believers and builders. The ten chapters of the book become ten modules, taught live, with the rhythm and accountability that make the covenant stick.

The founding cohort opens with the book

What’s inside

  • Ten modules, taught live by Ken. One a month, straight from the book.
  • A monthly Mastermind call. Teaching, hot seats and questions, with the whole room.
  • The 5 A.M. Circle. Small accountability pods that keep the morning covenant together.
  • The Five-Account Scorecard. An honest monthly look at God, health, relationships, time and finances.
  • A private community. Wins, questions and connections for the days between calls.
  • Quarterly workshops. Practical deep dives on AI, pricing and systems.
