Wisdom Is Currency

Privacy & text terms

Last updated October 15, 2026

Wisdom Is Currency is an economic empowerment ministry of Greater Works Church, led by Minister Ken Bridgett. This page explains what we collect when you sign up, how we use it, and how texts from us work.

What we collect

When you sign up we collect your name, phone number, email address, and anything you choose to tell us about what you would build. If you take part in the 7-Day Challenge, we also receive the journal answers you choose to send.

How we use it

  • To send you the details for our Thursday gatherings, recordings, and ministry updates.
  • To text you reminders, only if you checked the box agreeing to texts.
  • To get to know the people we serve, so the teaching meets you where you are.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We don't share it with anyone outside the ministry except the service providers that store our contact list and send our emails and texts for us, and only so they can do that.

Text message terms

  • Texts are optional. You only get texts from us if you check the box agreeing to them. Signing up doesn't require it.
  • What we send: reminders for the Thursday gatherings and ministry updates from Wisdom Is Currency (Minister Ken Bridgett).
  • How often: message frequency varies, usually a few messages a month.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any text and you won't get any more. Reply HELP for help, or email us.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Email

Every email from us includes a link to unsubscribe. You can also just reply and ask us to take you off the list.

Questions or changes

To update or remove your information, email Ken@SignatureBrandMarketing.com.
